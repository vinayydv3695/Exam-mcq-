# L14 — Case Study: Critically Endangered Vultures

![[assets/icons/14-vultures.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit2 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Why vultures matter
- Major **scavengers**.
- Consume carcasses, help prevent disease spread and maintain environmental hygiene.
- Lecture links conservation to ecological balance and public health.

## Main causes of decline
### Diclofenac
- Veterinary drug used in livestock.
- Vulture feeding on contaminated carcasses can suffer **kidney failure and death**.

### Other causes
- Habitat loss from deforestation/urbanization.
- Food scarcity from changes in livestock carcass disposal.
- Poisoning from pesticides or poisoned carcasses.
- Collisions/electrocution involving power lines and wind turbines.

## Conservation measures
- Ban veterinary use of **diclofenac**.
- Promote safer alternatives such as **meloxicam**.
- Captive breeding + release.
- **Vulture Safe Zones**.
- Awareness and wild-population monitoring.
- Protect nesting habitats and enforce against illegal poisoning.

## Importance of vultures
- Help limit disease spread including anthrax and rabies in the lecture.
- Rapid carcass removal maintains ecosystem hygiene.
- Can reduce feral-dog/scavenger populations that may contribute to disease transmission.
- Support biodiversity and ecosystem health.

## Fast recall
**Diclofenac → contaminated carcass → kidney failure → vulture death.**
