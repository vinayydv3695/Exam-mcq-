# L13 — Conservation of Biodiversity

![[assets/icons/13-conservation.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit2 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Conservation
- Protection, preservation, management or restoration of wildlife and natural resources.
- Main aim: ensure survival of species/habitats threatened by human activity and restore degraded ecosystems.

## Two approaches
### In-situ conservation = on-site
- Genetic resources protected **within natural populations in native habitats**.
- Focus: protect species, habitats and natural evolutionary processes.

### In-situ frameworks in India
**National Parks**
- Strictly reserved for wildlife betterment.
- Forestry, livestock grazing, cultivation and private ownership rights are prohibited in the lecture framing.
- Boundaries well marked; emphasis can be on a target species.
- Examples: Corbett, Dudhwa, Gir.

**Wildlife Sanctuaries**
- Protected mainly for animal conservation.
- Controlled timber/minor forest-product collection and private rights can be allowed if they do not harm wildlife.
- Boundaries/interference are less strict than national parks in the supplied notes; controlled tourism may occur.
- Examples: Ghana Bird Sanctuary, Hazaribag, Mudumalai.

**Biosphere reserves & sacred natural sites**
- Larger landscape-level conservation plus culturally protected forests/lakes.

### Ex-situ conservation = off-site
- Biodiversity components preserved **outside natural habitats** using specialized facilities.

| Technique | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Seed gene banks | Cold storage of seeds |
| Gene banks | Preserve genetic variability |
| Cryopreservation | Gametes/embryos/tissues in liquid nitrogen at very low temperature |
| Tissue culture banks | Virus-free plant propagation using cultured tissues |
| Captive breeding | Breed endangered species under controlled conditions |
| Botanical/zoological gardens | Living collections for education/research/recreation |
| Animal translocation | Move animals to safer regions |

## In-situ vs ex-situ
- **In-situ:** natural habitat; ecosystem-level; natural evolution.
- **Ex-situ:** outside habitat; artificial management; storage/breeding/research.

## Values of biodiversity
- **Social:** culture, religion, livelihoods, community protection.
- **Economic:** food, wood, fuel, industrial/medical resources.
- **Ethical:** “live and let live”; responsibility to other species.
- **Aesthetic:** beauty, tourism, arts/literature.
- **Ecological:** air/water purification, soil processes, pest control and planetary stability.

## MCQ trap
**In-situ = in original habitat. Ex-situ = outside original habitat.**
