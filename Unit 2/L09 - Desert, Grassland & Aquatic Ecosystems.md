# L09 — Desert, Grassland & Aquatic Ecosystems

![[assets/icons/10-desert-aquatic.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit2 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Desert ecosystem
- Ecological desert condition: **PET > precipitation** with sparse biological growth.
- Global coverage in the supplied lecture: about **17% of global land**, mainly belts between **15° and 35° N/S**.
- Indian distribution: Thar/Rajasthan and arid/semi-arid areas of Gujarat, Haryana, Punjab, MP, AP, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, UP and J&K/Ladakh.
- Annual rainfall: **<25 cm/year**; strong diurnal temperature variation.

### Xerophytes
- **Drought escaping/ephemeral:** complete life cycle in 4–6 weeks after rain (Scilla).
- **Drought evading:** small plants; 4–6 month lifespan (Argemone, Solanum).
- **Drought enduring:** woody shrubs/trees reduce leaves/leaflets (Acacia nilotica, Capparis decidua).
- **Drought resisting/succulents:** water storage in modified stems/phylloclades or fleshy leaves (Opuntia, cacti, Bryophyllum, Aloe, Agave).

### Consumers
- Primary: camel, sheep, wild ass.
- Secondary: desert cat, desert fox, toads, Great Indian Bustard, Florican.
- Tertiary: monitors, snakes, wolves.
- Decomposers: drought-tolerant fungi and bacteria.

## Cold desert / tundra
- Permafrost ~10–20 cm below the surface in the supplied notes; summer thaw becomes waterlogged.
- Producers: lichens, mosses, dwarf Betula and Salix.
- Consumers listed: reindeer, yak, polar deer, musk deer, antelope, penguins, polar bears, polar wolves.

## Desert services/threats
- Grazing/browsing, Jowar/Bajra, genetic reservoir for drought tolerance.
- Threats: nuclear testing, canal irrigation → evaporation and salinization/Oosar soil, tube-well over-extraction.
- **Bishnoi movement:** protection of Khejri trees and Blackbuck; lecture calls it an ancestral precursor to Chipko.

## Grasslands
- Rainfall **25–75 cm/year**; enough to prevent desert formation but not dense forest formation.
- Indian coverage in lecture: **18–19%**; ~250 million tons dry grass/hay/year.
- Temperate examples: Prairies, Steppes, Pampas, Tussock.
- Indian grasses: Dichanthium, Sehima, Phragmites, Saccharum, Cynodon, Cymbopogon.
- C4 grasses in lecture fix CO₂ efficiently at low concentration and have high water efficiency.
- Threats: conversion, overgrazing, poaching, genetic erosion.
- Conservation: protected areas, rotational grazing, fire control.

## Aquatic ecosystems
Key controls: **salinity, light penetration, dissolved oxygen, thermal dynamics, current/flow**.

### Classification by salinity
- **Freshwater:** <0.5 ppt; lentic + lotic.
- **Marine:** ~35 ppt; covers ~71% Earth surface in lecture.
- **Estuarine/brackish:** 0.5–35 ppt; mixing zones where rivers meet ocean.

### Lentic zones
- **Littoral:** shallow, sunlit shore; rooted plants.
- **Limnetic:** open, well-lit water; plankton/nekton.
- **Profundal:** deep, dark; detritivores and anaerobic decomposers.
- **Benthic:** bottom substrate; benthos.

### Thermal layers
- Epilimnion = warm, mixed, oxygen-rich surface.
- Thermocline/metalimnion = rapid temperature change with depth.
- Hypolimnion = cold, dense, deep layer; may become oxygen-depleted.

### Lotic continuum
- Headwaters/rithron: cold, fast, high DO, low primary production, CPOM from leaves.
- Lowland/potamon: warmer, wider, more turbid, higher primary production, phytoplankton + FPOM.

### Aquatic life forms
- **Plankton:** drifting organisms; phytoplankton + zooplankton.
- **Nekton:** active swimmers.
- **Periphyton:** attached to submerged surfaces.
- **Neuston:** surface-film organisms.
- **Benthos:** bottom-associated organisms.

### Estuaries
- Partially enclosed coastal water where river and seawater mix.
- Nutrient trap + fluctuating salinity + euryhaline organisms.
- Threat: eutrophication → algal blooms → bacterial decomposition → high BOD → hypoxic dead zones.
- Other threats: POPs/heavy metals, ocean acidification.
- Conservation: Ramsar Convention (1971), Montreux Record, Marine Protected Areas.
