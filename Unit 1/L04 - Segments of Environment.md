# L04 — Segments of Environment

![[assets/icons/04-atmosphere.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit1 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Four segments
1. **Atmosphere**
2. **Lithosphere**
3. **Hydrosphere**
4. **Biosphere**

## Atmosphere layers
| Layer | Key point | Temperature trend |
|---|---|---|
| Troposphere | Weather/clouds; we live here | Decreases with altitude |
| Stratosphere | Ozone layer | Increases with altitude |
| Mesosphere | Meteors burn; very thin air | Decreases with altitude |
| Thermosphere | Very high temperatures; high-energy solar radiation | Increases with altitude |

### Troposphere
- About **11 km** thick on average; thinner at poles, thicker at equator.
- Heated mainly from Earth’s surface.
- Cooling with altitude is linked in the lecture to lower pressure, adiabatic expansion and less near-surface heat trapping.

### Stratosphere
- About **11–48 km**.
- Ozone absorbs UV radiation and converts it to heat.
- Hence temperature rises with altitude.

### Mesosphere
- Temperature can reach about **−90°C** at the top.
- Little ozone, very thin air and strong radiative heat loss.

### Thermosphere
- Starts around **55 km** in the lecture.
- Oxygen, nitrogen and helium absorb EUV/X-rays.
- Ionization and high-energy radiation drive very high temperatures.

## Lithosphere / Earth structure
- Lithosphere = rigid outer Earth made of crust + upper rigid mantle; lecture gives ~70–100 km.
- **Crust:** ~5–70 km; continental **Sial** = silica + aluminium; oceanic **Sima** = silica + magnesium.
- **Mantle:** ~2,900 km; upper mantle + lower mantle.
- **Outer core:** ~2,200 km; molten iron + nickel; magnetic field generation.
- **Inner core:** radius ~1,220 km; solid iron + nickel.

## Hydrosphere
- Includes oceans, seas, rivers, streams, glaciers and other water bodies.
- Lecture values: ~1,360 million km³ total water; 97% salty ocean water; 2% glaciers/ice caps; 1% liquid freshwater available for human use.

## Water cycle
**Evaporation → Transpiration → Condensation → Precipitation → Collection/Runoff/Infiltration**
- Evapotranspiration = evaporation + plant transpiration.

## Biosphere
- Thin shell where atmosphere, lithosphere and hydrosphere overlap and life exists.
- Organisms obtain resources and return waste to the environment.
