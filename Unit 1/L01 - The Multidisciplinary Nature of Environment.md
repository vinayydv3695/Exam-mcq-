# L01 — The Multidisciplinary Nature of Environment

![[assets/icons/01-environment.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit1 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Core definition
- **Environment** = functional surroundings in which organisms live; includes elements needed directly or indirectly for survival.
- **Biotic components:** living — animals, plants, microorganisms.
- **Abiotic components:** non-living — soil, air, water, sunlight.

## Why environmental studies is multidisciplinary
Environmental issues are complex because organisms are affected by physical, biological, social and cultural conditions. Environmental studies therefore combines:
- **Life & physical sciences:** ecology, biology, chemistry, atmospheric science, oceanography, geology.
- **Humanities & social sciences:** anthropology, archaeology, sociology, history, political science, ethics.
- **Applied sciences:** engineering, economics.

## Five core objectives
1. **Knowledge** — understand ecosystems, natural interactions and vulnerabilities.
2. **Attitude of concern** — develop values and concern for Earth.
3. **Quality evaluation** — judge environmental programmes using social, economic, ecological and political factors.
4. **Protection & skill development** — identify, analyse and solve environmental problems.

## Scope: Conservation, Control, Replacement
| Pillar | Focus | Key actions |
|---|---|---|
| Conservation | Natural resources + biodiversity | Habitat restoration, wildlife/resource mapping, protect genetic variation |
| Control | Pollution + population pressure | Emission controls, manage urban sprawl/ecological footprints |
| Replacement | Sustainable development + eco-innovation | 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle; balance growth with green practices |

## Student-level environmental actions
- Replace single-use plastics with reusable alternatives.
- Reduce energy use; prefer efficient bulbs and natural ventilation where suitable.
- Avoid food waste; prefer reuse/swap over unnecessary fast-fashion consumption.
- Walk/cycle for short trips when practical.
- Prefer recyclable or rechargeable batteries and dispose of e-waste responsibly.

## MCQ traps
- **Biotic = living; abiotic = non-living.**
- Environmental studies is not only “biology”; it explicitly integrates social sciences, humanities and applied sciences.
- The 3Rs belong to the **Replacement/sustainable development** idea in the supplied lecture.
