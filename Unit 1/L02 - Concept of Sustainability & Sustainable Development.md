# L02 — Concept of Sustainability & Sustainable Development

![[assets/icons/02-sustainability.svg|64]]

**Tags:** #unit1 #mcq #environmental-studies

## Sustainability
- **Sustainability:** meeting present needs without sacrificing the ability of future generations to meet their needs.
- Three interconnected dimensions: **environmental, social, economic**.
- Nested idea: the **economy depends on society, and both depend on a healthy environment**.
- The lecture links sustainability to pollution, biodiversity loss, eutrophication and climate change.

## Three pillars
- **Environmental sustainability:** responsible use and protection of natural resources; healthy/resilient ecosystems.
- **Social sustainability:** equity, justice and essential needs of people/communities.
- **Economic sustainability:** stable, prosperous economy that supports society while reducing environmental harm.

## Sustainable development strategies
### Wind energy
- Wind turbines convert **kinetic energy of wind** into electricity.
- Renewable, plentiful and operationally clean in the lecture framing.
- Technology development can lower costs and improve generation ability.

### CNG
- Used as a cleaner fuel, especially in public transport examples such as Delhi.
- Lecture highlights: lead- and sulfur-free, high auto-ignition temperature, low operating cost, good air mixing, and reduced harmful emissions.
- Supplied figures: roughly **66% lower operating cost than petrol** and **28% lower than diesel** in Delhi.

### Solar energy
- PV systems use the **photovoltaic effect** to convert sunlight into electricity.
- Plentiful, decentralized, and clean during operation.
- Uses mentioned: homes, businesses, water heating and outdoor lighting.
- Indian companies mentioned: **Tata Power Solar** and **Adani Solar**.

### Bio-composting
- Controlled biological decomposition of organic matter by microorganisms.
- Produces a humus-like, nutrient-rich material.
- Benefits: soil fertility, nutrient cycling, better structure/porosity/water movement and beneficial microbial communities.

## 17 SDGs
Know the names in sequence: No Poverty; Zero Hunger; Good Health and Well-Being; Quality Education; Gender Equality; Clean Water and Sanitation; Affordable and Clean Energy; Decent Work and Economic Growth; Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure; Reduced Inequalities; Sustainable Cities and Communities; Responsible Consumption and Production; Climate Action; Life Below Water; Life on Land; Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions; Partnerships for the Goals.

## MCQ traps
- Sustainability is **not only environmental**.
- Wind = kinetic energy of wind; PV = sunlight to electricity.
- Bio-composting is microbial decomposition under controlled conditions.
